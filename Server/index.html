Trust

          
Zero-Knowledge Privacy

          
Your identity is encrypted on your device. We store only what we cannot read.

          
“I am personally unable to view any PII on this server, and neither is anyone else. Your email is sealed on your device before it reaches us, and is only ever opened by an isolated service — for a payment, a license key, or your own sign-in.”
— J.J. Warne, MilkandGin

          

            

              
1 · Your device

              
Holds the key

              
A key is derived from your passphrase (Argon2id) and never leaves. Your email is encrypted here before upload; login decrypts it here.

            

            

              
2 · Syxx server

              
Holds only ciphertext

              
Stores a blind index (for lookup) and sealed PII. No key, no plaintext. The operator sees ciphertext and nothing else.

            

            

              
3 · Third parties

              
Unseal in isolation

              
The PII is sealed to the payment processor and license mailer. It is decrypted only inside their isolated enclave — never by the app server.

            

          

          

            
What this guarantees

            

              
✓A database dump reveals no emails — only blind indexes and ciphertext.

              
✓The operator has no decryption key and cannot read your identity.

              
✓Plaintext exists only transiently, inside an isolated service, at the moment a third party genuinely needs it.

              
✓Your DNS reports and license key are never linked to your identity on the server.

            

          

          

            
Telemetry & Usage Data

            

              
Syxx collects two kinds of anonymous telemetry, both of which you can turn off in Settings → Privacy:

              
•Dimension ping (syxx.app) — Once per app launch: your OS name, OS version, and app version. No IP address, no device ID, no DNS data.

              
•Operational telemetry (bugsmasher.syxx.app) — OTel spans and Sentry crash reports. Spans contain probe labels, timing metrics, and DNS resolver classification. Domain names are never emitted. Resolver IPs are redacted to class tokens. Crash reports include stack traces but no personal data.

              
Data retention: OTel traces are retained for 30 days. Sentry crash reports are retained for 90 days. Data is stored on Syxx-owned infrastructure and is never shared with third parties.

              
Legal basis: Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time by turning telemetry off in Settings.

              
Your rights: Access, rectification, erasure, and portability. Contact us to exercise these rights. Because telemetry is anonymous, we may need additional information to locate your data.

              
Cookies & storage: This site uses localStorage for session management. No third-party cookies or trackers. The session token is cleared when you sign out.

              
Data controller: Joel J. Warne, d/b/a MilkandGin. Contact: privacy@syxx.app.
