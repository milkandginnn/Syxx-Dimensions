2. What Syxx does, and what it does not promise

Syxx inspects DNS resolution from your device and reports what it observed. It is a diagnostic instrument, not a guarantee.

We do not warrant that Syxx will detect any particular instance of DNS tampering, hijacking, spoofing, interception, or censorship, that it will detect all such activity, or that any result it produces is complete or free from error. Network measurement is inherently partial: evidence can be unavailable, degraded, delayed, or actively withheld by the network under test, and a party able to interfere with DNS may also be able to interfere with the detection of that interference.

Syxx is built to say so when that happens. Where the evidence does not support a Conclusion, the Service is designed to report the result as inconclusive rather than to report that your resolution is clean. An inconclusive result is correct behaviour of the Service, not a defect. Equally, a result reporting that nothing was detected means exactly that, that nothing was detected, and is not a statement that your network is safe.
