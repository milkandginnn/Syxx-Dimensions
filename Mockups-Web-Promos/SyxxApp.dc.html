About Syxx
“When all the tales were told, the Conclusion weighed them together and pronounced whether the network’s eyes could be believed.”
This app was inspired by DNS Monitor by Patrick Wardle at Objective-See. In my own suspicions that my network had been compromised, I couldn’t help but wish I had a tool that could actually (and quickly) interpret those reports for me. I was working on two larger products at the time and sadly had to put them aside, and during June 2026, began the journey to tell this story.
Big things are on the horizon as we chisel out the long-term purpose of the web-server component. We’re currently planning for it to serve as an authoritative ground-truth oracle, an inside/outside differential (“diff”) endpoint, a DNS trace/hierarchy diagnostic, a historical drift tool, an outside-in resolver check, and a crowd-intelligence source.
While I did not use his products or code, I want to thank Mr. Wardle for his efforts in creating and distributing such great network security at no cost. (I endeavored to do the same; alas, I’m poor and server space is expensive.) And I want to thank all of you for giving this product a try. Use the app, visit us on Syxx.app, and be a part of our story so that we can be a part of yours.
—MilkandGin
