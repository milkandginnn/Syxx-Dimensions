What you'll be testing
Nnyx
Rootkit & threat scanner · macOS Tahoe

A security control plane for your Mac. Nnyx runs a multi-engine sweep — baseline hashing, threat signatures, and behavioral pattern rules — to surface rootkits, malware, and stealth persistence. Every finding is framed plainly: what it saw, the evidence, what Nnyx already did, and what you should do next. Its governed trust subsystem keeps auto-derived known-good binaries separate from your own trust overrides, and every change is admin-authenticated, audited, and reversible.

Zzyn
Network monitor & outbound firewall · macOS

See — and decide — exactly what your Mac talks to. Zzyn maps every process to its live connections, shows flows, bytes, and destinations in real time, and blocks trackers and unwanted endpoints at the filter. When a new app reaches out, you get a clear prompt with full provenance, so you can allow or deny with confidence. Community-verified rules and per-process profiles keep the noise down and the control in your hands.

Sound complicated?

Not to worry. Zzyn helps with the heavy lifting by using skill-adaptive controls to automate processes for those with less experience. You're free to take control whenever you like — simply by adjusting your skill level.
